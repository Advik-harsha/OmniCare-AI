# Plan 03-01: Contactless Camera rPPG Vitals & Hemodynamic Shock Index

**Goal:** Implement contactless camera photoplethysmography (POS-Net INT8 on Qualcomm Hexagon NPU) extracting vital signs in 8.2ms, hemodynamic shock index, and streaming PPG waveforms.

## User Story
**As a** triage nurse or emergency clinician,  
**I want to** measure patient heart rate, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, and shock index contactlessly within 10 seconds via the webcam,  
**so that** infection transmission risk is eliminated and deteriorating patients are triaged instantly.

    1. Implement `backend/engine/qnn_rppg.py` using dual-import pattern:
       - Facial ROI tracker modeling HP True Vision 5MP camera feed with forehead and malar cheek extraction.
       - Plane-Orthogonal-to-Skin (POS) rPPG chrominance projection algorithm isolating blood volume pulse (BVP).
       - Quantitative vitals computation:
         * Heart Rate (HR: 45 - 180 bpm, target accuracy &plusmn;1.4 bpm)
         * Oxygen Saturation (SpO2: 85 - 100%, calibration against ratio-of-ratios)
         * Respiratory Rate (RR: 8 - 40 breaths/min derived from respiratory sinus arrhythmia)
         * Heart Rate Variability (HRV SDNN: ms)
       - Hemodynamic Shock Index (SI = HR / SBP):
         * Normal: 0.5 - 0.7
         * Mild/Caution: 0.7 - 0.9
         * Moderate Shock: 0.9 - 1.1
         * Severe Shock: &gt; 1.1
       - PPG waveform generator producing calibrated cyan pulse points for live 60 FPS HTML5 canvas rendering.
       - Preset patient vitals simulation for static/offline demonstration.
    2. Mount REST endpoints in `backend/main.py`:
       - `GET /api/vitals/rppg/live` (returns live frame with instantaneous PPG points, HR, SpO2, RR, and Shock Index).
       - `POST /api/vitals/rppg/analyze` (accepts camera stream or preset, returns full hemodynamic profile).
