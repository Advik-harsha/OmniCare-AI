# Plan 03-02: 12-Lead Paper ECG Digitizer & PTB-XL Arrhythmia AI

**Goal:** Implement 12-lead paper ECG digitization with 98.4% optical grid suppression, PTB-XL INT8 arrhythmia classification in 6.8ms, and electrophysiological interval calculations.

## User Story
**As a** general practitioner in a rural clinic receiving a paper 12-lead ECG printout from a legacy machine,  
**I want to** take a photo of the paper strip and have OmniCare AI remove the grid, extract the raw voltage signal, and detect STEMI or AFib within 7ms,  
**so that** life-saving thrombolysis or cardiac transfer can be initiated immediately.
