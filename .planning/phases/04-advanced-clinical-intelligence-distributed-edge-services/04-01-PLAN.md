# Plan 04-01: NEWS2 Calculator, PMBJP Jan Aushadhi Generics & CYP450 DDIs

**Goal:** Implement automated Royal College of Physicians NEWS2 clinical deterioration scoring, PMBJP Jan Aushadhi generic drug substitution with 82.9% patient savings, and CYP450 drug-drug interaction contraindication checking.

## User Story
**As a** primary care physician in a community health center,  
**I want to** automatically calculate NEWS2 deterioration risk and substitute branded prescriptions with affordable Jan Aushadhi generic alternatives while screening for dangerous drug interactions,  
**so that** patient safety is prioritized and medication costs are reduced by over 80%.

## Tasks

  <name>Implement Royal College of Physicians NEWS2 Calculator</name>
  <files>
    backend/engine/news2_calculator.py,
    backend/main.py
  </files>
  <action>
    1. Implement `backend/engine/news2_calculator.py` using dual-import pattern:
       - 7 Royal College of Physicians physiological parameters: Respiration Rate, SpO2 (Scale 1/2), Supplemental Oxygen, Systolic Blood Pressure, Pulse Rate, Consciousness (AVPU), Temperature.
       - Individual parameter scores (0 to 3 points per parameter) and total cumulative score (0 to 20).
       - Clinical risk stratification: Low (0-4), Low-Medium (single 3-point score), Medium (5-6), High (7+).
       - Recommended clinical escalation protocol (Ward nurse review, Urgent doctor bedside review, Emergency ICU team activation).
       - Shock index computation (HR / SBP).
    2. Mount REST endpoints in `backend/main.py`:
       - `POST /api/clinical/news2/calculate` (accepts vital signs, returns score breakdown and escalation pathway).
  </action>

  </verify>
  <done>NEWS2 calculator delivers standard score breakdown and clinical escalation pathways.</done>

    1. Implement `backend/engine/drug_guardian.py` using dual-import pattern:
       - PMBJP Jan Aushadhi database mapping top Indian branded medications to generic chemical equivalents:
         * Augmentin 625mg (Amoxicillin + Clavulanate) -> ₹210 vs ₹42 (80.0% savings)
         * Lipitor / Atorva 20mg (Atorvastatin) -> ₹185 vs ₹28 (84.9% savings)
         * Plavix / Clopilet 75mg (Clopidogrel) -> ₹160 vs ₹24 (85.0% savings)

       - Average savings calculator proving 82.9% patient savings.
       - Cytochrome P450 (CYP450) drug-drug interaction (DDI) engine checking CYP3A4, CYP2C19, CYP2D6 contraindications:

         * Atorvastatin + Clarithromycin (CYP3A4 inhibition increases rhabdomyolysis risk)

       - `POST /api/drugs/jan_aushadhi/substitute` (accepts branded drug list, returns generic matches, cost comparison, and savings %).
       - `POST /api/drugs/interactions/check` (accepts co-prescribed drugs, returns CYP450 contraindications, severity, and clinical alternatives).
