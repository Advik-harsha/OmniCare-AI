# Plan 04-02: Council of AI Specialists & Handheld POCUS Ultrasound AI

**Goal:** Implement autonomous Multi-Agent Council of AI Specialists with Chief Medical Officer (CMO) arbitration, and Handheld Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS) AI for cardiac LVEF and pleural sliding evaluation.

## User Story
**As a** rural health clinician handling complex diagnostic presentations,  
**I want to** summon an autonomous multi-disciplinary council of AI specialists for peer consensus and analyze point-of-care ultrasound clips for cardiac function and acute pneumothorax,  
**so that** I have immediate specialist-tier decision support directly on-device without internet connectivity.

    1. Implement `backend/engine/pocus_ultrasound.py` using dual-import pattern:
       - Cardiac POCUS AI (Parasternal Long Axis / Apical 4-Chamber):
         * End-diastolic volume (EDV) and End-systolic volume (ESV) segmentation.
         * Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF %) calculation using Simpson's biplane approximation.
         * Functional grading: Normal (LVEF >= 55%), Mild Dysfunction (45-54%), Moderate Dysfunction (30-44%), Severe Dysfunction (<30%).
         * Stroke volume (SV) and cardiac output estimation.
       - Pulmonary / Pleural POCUS AI:
         * Pleural line detection and M-mode analysis.
         * Dynamic sign identification: "Seashore Sign" (granular sandy pattern below pleural line indicating normal sliding) vs "Stratosphere / Barcode Sign" (parallel horizontal lines indicating absent lung sliding / pneumothorax).
         * B-line interstitial artifact counter: <3 (normal aeration), >=3 (interstitial syndrome / pulmonary edema).
       - Simulated probe feed generator delivering structured frame arrays for UI rendering.
    2. Mount REST endpoints in `backend/main.py`:
       - `POST /api/pocus/cardiac/ejection_fraction` (accepts ultrasound frames/metrics, returns LVEF %, volumes, and diagnostic category).
       - `POST /api/pocus/lung/sliding_sign` (accepts M-mode frame/metrics, returns pleural sliding status, Seashore vs Barcode sign, and B-line count).
