# Plan 02-01: Dermatology & Retinal Screening with Monk Skin Tone & Explainable ABCD

**Goal:** Build on-device vision diagnostic AI for Dermatology & Retinal screening with optical IQA, Monk Skin Tone calibration (MST 1-10), and explainable ABCD / Grad-CAM visual heatmaps.

## User Story
**As a** field clinician evaluating cutaneous lesions or retinal imaging in rural health camps,  
**I want** fast on-device lesion segmentation calibrated for diverse Indian skin phototypes with explainable clinical metrics,  
**so that** I can identify suspicious melanomas or diabetic retinopathy early without cloud connectivity.

## Tasks

<task type="tracer">
  <name>Implement Explainable ABCD Engine & Optical IQA</name>
  <files>
    backend/engine/xai_abcd.py
  </files>
  <action>
    1. Implement `backend/engine/xai_abcd.py` providing:
       - Optical Image Quality Assessment (IQA: sharpness score, glare detection, lighting adequacy, resolution validation).
       - Monk Skin Tone (MST 1-10) calibration factor calculating epidermal melanin correction curves.
       - Explainable ABCD rule scoring:
         * A (Asymmetry Index: 0.0 to 1.0)
         * B (Border Irregularity score: 0 to 8)
         * C (Color Variegation: count of 6 suspicious hues: black, dark brown, light brown, red, white, blue)
         * D (Diameter measurement in mm)
         * Total Dermoscopy Score (TDS = 1.3*A + 0.1*B + 0.5*C + 0.5*D).
       - Grad-CAM visual attention coordinates for lesion heatmap visualization.
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>python -c "from backend.engine.xai_abcd import evaluate_abcd_rule, compute_optical_iqa; iqa = compute_optical_iqa([100, 150, 200]); abcd = evaluate_abcd_rule(asymmetry=0.4, border=3, colors=['dark_brown', 'black'], diameter_mm=6.5, mst_scale=6); assert abcd['tds_score'] > 0; assert iqa['quality_passed'] is True; print('ABCD XAI OK')"</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>xai_abcd.py evaluates clinical ABCD metrics, optical IQA, and melanin calibration.</done>
</task>
