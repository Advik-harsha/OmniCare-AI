# Plan 02-02: HP Poly Studio Pulmonary Stethoscopy & YAMNet INT8 Engine

**Goal:** Build on-device acoustic diagnostic engine modeling HP Poly Studio AI beamforming microphones, chest friction suppression, late-inspiratory phase gating, and YAMNet INT8 breath sound classification.

## User Story
**As a** rural health clinician auscultating a pediatric or elderly respiratory patient,  
**I want** on-device pulmonary acoustics analysis with friction noise suppression and automated classification of wheezes and crackles,  
**so that** I can diagnose COPD, pneumonia, or asthma exacerbation in noisy clinic environments.
