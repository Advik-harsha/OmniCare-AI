# Plan 02-03: Whisper-Small INT8 Dictation & Llama-3.2-3B INT4 Clinical SOAP Scribing

**Goal:** Build on-device speech-to-text dictation with Whisper-Small INT8 and autonomous clinical scribing with Llama-3.2-3B INT4 generating structured SOAP notes and WHO ICD-10-CM diagnostic codes.

## User Story
**As a** busy physician conducting rapid clinical consultations,  
**I want to** speak naturally and have an on-device AI transcribe and synthesize consultation dialogue into a structured SOAP clinical note with ICD-10 codes,  
**so that** documentation overhead is minimized without sending voice recordings to external cloud servers.
